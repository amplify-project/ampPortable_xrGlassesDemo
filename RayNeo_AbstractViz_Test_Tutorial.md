# RayNeo XR Score Viewer Controls

Use the right temple touchpad on the RayNeo glasses to control the app.

## Start

1. Put on the RayNeo glasses.
2. Open the XR Score Viewer app.
3. Wait until the visual appears in front of you.

## Change the Visualization

1. Single tap the right temple touchpad.
2. The app changes to the other visualization.
3. Single tap again to switch back.

## Make the Visual Smaller or Larger

1. Look directly at the visual you want to resize.
2. Swipe forward on the right temple touchpad to make it smaller.
3. Swipe backward on the right temple touchpad to make it larger.
4. Repeat until the visual feels comfortable to view.

If the size does not change, keep looking at the visual and try the swipe again.

## Move the Visual Up or Down

1. Swipe up on the right temple touchpad to raise the visual.
2. Swipe down on the right temple touchpad to lower the visual.
3. Repeat until the visual is at a comfortable height.

## Quit the App

1. Double tap the right temple touchpad.
2. The app closes.

## Quick Reference

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Change visualization | Single tap |
| Make smaller | Look at the visual, then swipe forward |
| Make larger | Look at the visual, then swipe backward |
| Move up | Swipe up |
| Move down | Swipe down |
| Quit | Double tap |
